## Why Care?

Until now kay-os was a repo of mail scripts, and nothing in it said who it
was for. The splash says it plainly: kay-os is for people with seven inboxes,
four calendars, and a dozen involvements, often with ADHD or a 2e mind. It
says how the colony will help, and it's honest that one agent works today.

## What's New?

- **A splash at `lossless-group.github.io/kay-os/`**, deployed from `splash/`
  on every push to `main`.
- **The study, summarized.** [ADHD and Executive Function Skills](https://github.com/lossless-group/study-adhd-and-executive-function-skills)
  found that the nine skills it profiles help in three places (the reply, the
  session, state outside the chat) and none covers all three. The page shows
  that table and the row kay-os means to fill.
- **Seven house rules** every agent in the colony follows, each one from a
  pattern several authors in the study reached on their own: a two-minute
  first step, three choices at most, numeric triggers, parking tangents, scoring
  effort, consent before memory, and silence by default.
- **The colony roster.** The Archivist (mail into R2, every five minutes) is
  live. The Herald, a Hermes agent on the phone, is next. Gatekeeper,
  Timekeeper, Parking Attendant, and Steward are planned.
- **Its own look.** An arrivals board with split-flap tiles and status lamps,
  condensed display caps, and Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. Dark by
  default, with light and vibrant modes. Search, changelog, and context-v
  pages come with it.
