## Why Care?

A connector only proves itself when someone uses it. Michael added
`https://mcp.kay-os.lol/mcp` to the Claude app, signed in through id.didi.sh,
and handed Claude the archive. Claude found three real problems, Michael logged
them as issues, and each is fixed and deployed.

## What's New?

**The connector is in use.** The first sign-in was refused on purpose and
logged Michael's didi account; that account is now the only one allowed.
Claude negotiates MCP `2025-11-25` after trying `2026-07-28` first.

**Threads work on every account.** Mail the laptop synced had reached the
cloud without conversation ids, so threads came back empty on the work
accounts. The laptop's index had every id, so `mail/push_meta_to_d1.py` sent
its metadata (230,938 messages) to D1, and the backfill now fills ids on
anything indexed before its metadata arrived. humain.vc and mandatory.ventures
went from 1 and 0 to 714 of 714 each; Gmail to 44,092 of 44,100 so far.

**Search ranks the email about something first.** Keyword searches now sort
by relevance, with a word in the subject counting ten times one in the body,
and return a subject excerpt beside the body one, so it's visible why a result
matched. The tool says plainly that words are ANDed.

**Recent mail has previews**: the first line of each body, with quoted replies
stripped.

**Calendar tools in the connector** (plan C6): `list_meetings`,
`meetings_with`, `last_met`, `people_met`, and `create_event`, which writes to
the calendar through kay-os's own Google access only when Michael asks.
learnstart's history (9,242 events, 41,723 occurrences, 41,482 attendees) is in
D1, and every answer says how current its data is.

**Options written down**: Cloudflare AI Search, driven from the Agents SDK, is
now in the exploration and the attachments spec as the managed alternative for
search by meaning, to trial on humain's documents before building our own. The
connector spec notes the Agents SDK's `McpAgent` as an option for keeping up
with MCP's protocol changes.

## What's Next

Support MCP `2026-07-28` so Claude stops falling back. Live calendar sync in
the Worker (C5). A trial of AI Search. The same ranking and previews for the
laptop's `kaymail.py`.
