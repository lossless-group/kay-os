## Why Care?

The archive was searchable from Cloudflare but only by code. Now it has a
front door Claude can use from any device, signed in through the tree's own
identity service. And the calendars that say who Michael actually met are
landing next to the mail, starting with twelve years of LearnStart history.

## What's New?

**The connector is live at `https://mcp.kay-os.lol/mcp`.**

- Michael bought `kay-os.lol` through Cloudflare, so the connector Worker
  claims `mcp.kay-os.lol` as a custom domain; Cloudflare made the DNS and the
  certificate.
- Sign-in goes through **id.didi.sh**. The connector checks every token
  against id.didi.sh's public key: issuer, audience `https://mcp.kay-os.lol`,
  expiry, and Michael's didi account. id.didi.sh registered kay-os as a
  resource the same night.
- Four read-only tools, the same as the laptop's: `search_mail`,
  `read_message`, `list_attachments`, `get_attachment`. Files come as
  15-minute signed links the Worker serves from the original email.
- Everything an email's sender wrote comes back inside randomized
  untrusted-content markers, every call is logged, and the hard rule from
  `AGENTS.md` goes out to every client.
- Built by a subagent from plan C4, 31 tests. Still to do: the first sign-in
  from the Claude app, which sets the one didi account allowed in.

**Calendars as context.**

- A new spec, `Calendars-as-Context`: four sources (Google Takeout, kay-os's
  own Google access, Vimcal, and the invites in the mail) feeding one set of
  tables, `calendar_events`, `calendar_attendees`, and `calendar_occurrences`,
  with the same meeting tied together by its calendar UID.
- `calendar/import_takeout.py` imports a Takeout export. learnstart's: 9,242
  events across 13 calendars, 41,723 dated occurrences with every recurring
  series expanded, 41,482 attendee rows, in about 13 seconds.
- People met per year at LearnStart: 670 in 2013, 918 in 2015, 892 in 2020,
  379 in 2022.
- Exports are filed by account and date, and `mail/r2.sh push-calendar` keeps
  them in R2 under `calendar/`, add-only like raw mail.

**Four MCP servers in kay-os sessions**, listed in a new README section:
kaymail (the local archive), **Vimcal** (calendars across eight accounts),
**Superhuman** (live mail in every mailbox linked to it, one at a time), and
**macos-mcp** (control of the Mac). macos-mcp runs only on a Mac, through
`scripts/mcp-macos.sh`, which serves an empty server everywhere else, and
with its `standard` profile, because kay-os sessions read email anyone can
write.

## Found Along the Way

- **About 500 of learnstart's "events" are birthdays**: yearly all-day series
  starting between 1933 and 1989. Meeting counts filter on `all_day = 0`.
- **id.didi.sh only issues tokens for registered resources**, and its consent
  screen asks for an entity, so a new connector needs a line in its
  `oauth_resources` and a redeploy.
- **Claude wants the resource named exactly as typed** (`…/mcp`), while
  id.didi.sh's tokens name the registered origin. The connector advertises
  one and checks the other.
