## Why Care?

Most of what matters in email arrives as an attachment: the deck, the term
sheet, the signed contract. Sync has always downloaded every one, inside its
`.eml`, but nothing could find them. This round works out how to make them
searchable by keyword, by meaning, and by who sent what, without standing up a
data platform to do it. It also sets up a small inbox to try that on before
running it over 440,000 messages.

## What's New?

- **A mirror of today's storage**: original `.eml` files, one SQLite index, and
  a copy in R2, with the attachments already inside the raw files.
  `context-v/mirrors/Database-Setup-for-Email.md`.
- **An exploration of 2026 practice**, comparing where metadata lives, what
  format extracted text takes, which tool converts attachments, where vectors
  live, and how a graph gets built. A second pass weighs libSQL, Postgres with
  pgvector, and SurrealDB, records what running Graphiti on Neo4j actually cost
  us (about $0.76 an episode hosted), and moves the always-on side to
  Cloudflare: a Worker connector over D1, Vectorize, and R2, with Docling in a
  Cloudflare Container.
  `context-v/explorations/Attachments-Search-and-a-Knowledge-Graph.md`.
- **A spec in four phases**: an attachments inventory, extraction to Markdown,
  hybrid search, then a correspondent graph. It's a draft, waiting on sign-off
  and on the study below.
  `context-v/specs/Attachments-as-First-Class-Mail.md`.
- **A click-by-click playbook for connecting a Google Workspace mailbox**:
  admin settings, 2-Step Verification, All Mail's *Show in IMAP*, the app
  password, and the exact error each missed step produces.
  `context-v/blueprints/Connecting-a-Google-Workspace-Mailbox.md`.
- **A third mailbox, `michael@humain.vc`**, connected by following that
  playbook: 713 messages, 0.18 GB, fully synced in under a minute. It's the
  test bed for the attachments work.
- **Doppler, being set up** for the secrets kay-os runs on, chiefly so the
  Worker's account list stops being pasted by hand. The README covers the one
  environment kay-os needs and how to add a secret. Nothing reads from Doppler
  yet, and 1Password stays the source of truth.
- **A study, mounted at `studies/study-email-toolkits-agents`**: which projects
  are taming email with agents, starting with
  [msgvault](https://github.com/kenn-io/msgvault), so kay-os can decide whether
  to adopt, extend, or build.

## What's Next

Count humain.vc's attachments by type, size, and hash (Phase 1 of the spec, on
713 messages instead of 440,000). Read msgvault's code in the study before
building anything it already does.
