## Why Care?

The [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
(OKF) asks very little of a folder of markdown: parseable frontmatter, a
non-empty `type` on every doc, and a table of contents in `index.md`. Meet
that, and any OKF tool can read the folder without knowing anything about how
we work.

Context-v is now an OKF profile, and the rule that makes it one is a single
field: every doc declares a `type`, which is the name of its folder. This repo
is part of the tree-wide sweep that brings `context-v/` folders up to that
rule. It's also the repo's first changelog entry, so `changelog/` starts here.

## What's New?

- Both explorations in `context-v/explorations/` now end their frontmatter
  with `type: Explorations`. Nothing else in them moved, and no body line
  changed.
- `context-v/index.md` declares `okf_version: "0.2"` and lists the one folder.
- `context-v/explorations/index.md` lists both explorations, each with its
  lede as the one-line description.

## Under the Hood

This is a compliance change, not a content change, so the docs keep their
dates and versions. The edit was made by hand; no script wrote frontmatter.

The sweep's second phase, filling the kit's baseline frontmatter, found
nothing to do: both docs already carry `title`, `date_created`,
`date_modified`, `site_uuid`, `hex_code`, and `authors`, in the standard
shape. So there's no second entry.

The two explorations were sitting uncommitted in the working copy; they were
committed as written, in their own commit, just before this one.
